## About Armenia

Armenia is a small, mountainous country between Europe and Asia. It was the first country to make Christianity its official religion, back in 301 AD, and many holiday foods come from church traditions. People eat a lot of bread, herbs, cheese, grilled meat, and fruit, especially apricots. Bread is a big deal in Armenia. Lavash, their flatbread, is even on UNESCO's list of cultural heritage.

## The Dish

Gata is a sweet Armenian bread with a buttery, crumbly filling made from butter, sugar, and flour. There are a lot of regional versions, and it's often baked for holidays and family gatherings. Expect a soft, slightly sweet bread with a rich, sugary layer inside, a lot like a coffee cake. I decorated the tops with strips of dough.

## Review

A deliciously sweet bread with a paste-like filling. This was so good that we devoured all three loaves. Let it cool, though. It tasted a LOT better after it cooled compared to when it was hot. I didn't use nuts, but I wish I had. They would have improved the texture and flavor even more, not that it needed it.

## Gata

- Serves: 8 (3 loaves)
- Time: About 3 hours, plus cooling

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

#### Dough

- 3½ cups all-purpose flour
- 1 cup whole milk
- ¼ cup honey
- 2¼ tsp active dry yeast
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter, melted
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla extract

#### Filling

- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ cup all-purpose flour
- ⅓ cup walnuts, finely chopped (optional)
- Pinch of salt

#### Egg wash

- 1 egg
- 1 tsp milk

### Directions

1. Warm the milk, then add the honey and yeast. Set it aside for 5 minutes to let the yeast activate.
2. Add the rest of the dough ingredients. Mix and knead until soft and pliable. Let it rise 1 hour, until almost double in size.
3. Mix all of the filling ingredients together.
4. Divide the dough into 4 equal pieces.
5. Flatten 3 pieces into round circles, about ½ inch thick.
6. Put ⅓ of the filling right in the middle of each circle. Fold the circle over and seal the edges. Basically, you're trapping the filling inside the bread.
7. Roll the folded circle back into a circle, being careful not to tear the dough. I shot for just under ½ inch thick, which was about an 8-inch circle.
8. Roll the 4th piece into a 12–14 inch disc. Cut it into strips and use them to decorate the other breads. This is just decoration, so go wild.
9. Proof for 20 minutes until it's puffy.
10. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Mix the egg and milk to make an egg wash. Brush each of the 3 loaves.
11. Poke holes all over the top with a fork. This is important to keep it from bursting.
12. Bake 28–32 minutes until deep golden. Cool completely before eating.

Note: If you use nuts, toast the walnuts first. You could substitute pecans and it would be just as good.
