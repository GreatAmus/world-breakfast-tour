## The dish

Filhós are light, crispy fried dough fritters made with coconut milk and warm spices. They're used in a lot of Portuguese-influenced countries.

## What we thought

These ones have a distinctly Central African flavor with the banana and coconut.

## Rating

4/5

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

#### Fritters

- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp instant yeast
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- ¼ tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- ¾ cup coconut milk
- 1 egg
- 2 cups vegetable oil, for frying

#### To serve

- 2 tbsp powdered sugar
- 1 ripe banana, sliced

### Directions

1. Mix the flour, yeast, brown sugar, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg in a large bowl.
2. Add the coconut milk, vanilla, and egg. Mix until blended.
3. Cover the batter and rest for 30 minutes. The batter will be puffed up but extremely sticky.
4. Heat the oil to about 350°F in a frying pan. Test the heat with a small drop of batter. It should sizzle immediately and float to the surface.
5. Drop heaped tablespoons of batter into the oil in batches. Don't overcrowd. Fry 2–3 minutes per side until deep golden brown, turning once.
6. Drain on paper towels for 1 minute, then dust generously with powdered sugar. Add the bananas on top.
