## The dish

Filhós are light, crispy fried dough fritters found across the Portuguese-speaking world. Ours were made with coconut milk and warm spices, and the banana and coconut give them a distinctly Central African flavor.

## What we thought

Light, crispy, and well spiced. Rating: 4/5.

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

- 2 cups flour
- 1 tsp instant yeast
- 1 tbl brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- ¾ cups coconut milk
- 1 egg
- 2 cups vegetable oil (for frying)

### To serve

- 2 tbl powdered sugar
- 1 ripe banana, sliced

### Directions

1. Mix flour, yeast, brown sugar, salt, cinnamon, and nutmeg in a large bowl.
2. Add coconut milk, vanilla, and egg. Mix until blended.
3. Cover batter and rest for 30 minutes. The batter will be puffed up but extremely sticky.
4. Heat the oil to about 350°F in a frying pan. Test the heat with a small drop of batter. It should sizzle immediately and float to the surface.
5. Drop heaped tablespoons of batter into the oil in batches. Don't overcrowd. Fry 2–3 minutes per side until deep golden brown, turning once.
6. Drain on paper towels for 1 minute, then dust generously with powdered sugar. Add bananas on top.
