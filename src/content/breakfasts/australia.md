## The dish

Pikelets are small, thick pancakes, a classic Australian and New Zealand treat for breakfast or afternoon tea. Compared with American pancakes, they're sweeter, made with less butter, and less fluffy, and they're usually served with butter and jam. Thanks to Bluey, most kids already know what they are.

## What we thought

There might be more iconic Australian dishes, but you can't have a child under 10 and not make these. Rating: 4.7/5 (everyone loves pancakes).

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp golden syrup (or honey)
- 1 cup milk
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1 pinch of salt
- 2 tsp butter, melted

### Cook and serve

- Butter
- Good quality jam
- 1/2 cup heavy cream combined with 2 tbsp of powdered sugar and whipped to soft peaks (you could also buy a can of whipped cream)

### Directions

1. Mix the sugar, milk, eggs, melted butter, and honey together.
2. Mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together then add it to the sugar/egg mixture. Blend the two but don’t overmix it.
3. Grease a pan with butter over a low-medium heat.
4. Add about 2 tbsp of batter to the pan in a circle.
5. When bubbles appear on the surface of the batter, flip the pancake over. Cook until both sides are a light brown.
6. Serve with jam and whipped cream.
