## The dish

Pikelets are similar to American pancakes but are sweeter with less butter. American pancakes are fluffier as well. Bluey means that most kids know what these are.

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

#### Pikelets

- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp golden syrup (or honey)
- 1 cup milk
- 1½ cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tsp baking powder
- ¼ tsp baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 2 tsp butter, melted

#### To serve

- Butter
- Good-quality jam
- ½ cup heavy cream whipped to soft peaks with 2 tbsp powdered sugar (or a can of whipped cream)

### Directions

1. Mix the sugar, milk, egg, melted butter, and golden syrup together.
2. Mix the flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt together, then add it to the egg mixture. Blend the two, but don't overmix.
3. Grease a pan with butter over low-medium heat.
4. Add about 2 tbsp of batter to the pan in a circle.
5. When bubbles appear on the surface of the batter, flip the pancake over. Cook until both sides are a light brown.
6. Serve with jam and whipped cream.
