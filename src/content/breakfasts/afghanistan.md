## About Afghanistan

Afghanistan is a landlocked, mountainous country between Central and South Asia. It sat right on the old Silk Road, so traders brought in Persian, Indian, and Central Asian cooking. Most people eat rice, flatbread, and yogurt, with lamb, chicken, and lots of fresh herbs. Winters are hard and much of the land is too dry for big farms, so filling, simple foods like bread and potatoes stretch a long way.

## The Dish

Bolani are thin flatbreads stuffed with a filling, usually potato, leeks, or pumpkin, then cooked until crisp. They're cheap, filling, and easy to eat on the go, which is why they're one of the most popular street foods in Afghanistan. They're usually served with chaka, a garlicky yogurt dip. Expect a crisp outside, a soft, herby potato middle, and a cool, tangy dip to cut through it. Mine had a potato filling with green onion, cilantro, mint, and green chili, and I cooked them in the air fryer instead of frying them.

## Review

These were pretty bland. They just didn't have much flavor. They may have been better fried, since I made them in the air fryer to avoid eating so much fried dough. Hot sauce made a big difference. It brought the flavor from a 3 to a 4 for me. Adults gave it a 3.0/5 overall. Kids gave it a 2.0/5 and still didn't like it, even with hot sauce.


## Bolani (stuffed flatbread)

- Serves: 3 (2 bolani each)
- Time: About 1½ hours

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

#### Bread

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ¼ cup plain yogurt
- ⅛ cup warm water
- ¼ tsp salt

#### Filling

- 1 medium potato, boiled and mashed
- ½ medium onion, thinly sliced
- ½ tsp olive oil
- 1½ green onions, finely chopped
- ¼ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
- ⅛ cup fresh mint, chopped
- ½ green chili, finely chopped
- ¼ tsp ground cumin
- ¼ tsp ground coriander
- ⅛ tsp turmeric
- Zest of ¼ lemon
- ¼ tsp salt
- ½ garlic clove, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil, for brushing

#### Chaka (yogurt sauce)

- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- ½ garlic clove, minced
- ½ tsp dried mint
- ⅛ tsp salt

### Directions

1. Mix the bread ingredients. Knead 5 minutes until soft and elastic. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
2. Cook the onion in ½ tsp olive oil over low heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and sweet.
3. Combine the mashed potato, onion, green onions, cilantro, mint, green chili, cumin, coriander, turmeric, lemon zest, salt, and garlic. Mix well and taste for seasoning.
4. Mix the Greek yogurt, garlic, dried mint, and salt in a small bowl. Refrigerate until serving.
5. Divide the dough into 6 balls. Roll each as thin as possible on a lightly floured surface, about tortilla thickness. Spread filling over one half, fold it over, and press the edges firmly with a fork to seal.
6. Preheat the air fryer to 375°F for 5 minutes. Brush the bolani with oil. Cook in batches for 12 minutes, flipping at the 5-minute mark, until deeply golden and crispy.
7. Serve immediately with the chaka on the side for dipping.

Note: I made these in an air fryer to avoid so much fried dough. I highly recommend hot sauce.
