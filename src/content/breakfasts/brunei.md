## About Brunei

Brunei is a small, wealthy country on the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia. Most people are Malay and Muslim, and the country is ruled by a sultan. Their food is mostly Malay, with rice at almost every meal, coconut milk, fish, and plenty of chili. Rice and coconuts grow easily in the hot, wet climate, so they're in almost everything.

## The Dish

Nasi katok is a Bruneian comfort food of steamed rice with fried chicken and a spicy sambal. I found out the name refers to the traditional practice of buying the meal from a vendor who would "knock" (katok) on your door. It's cheap, quick, and sold all day and all night, which is why it's so popular. Expect crispy fried chicken, a sweet and spicy chili sauce, and plain rice to cool it down. I made coconut rice to go with mine.

## Review

I am a sucker for fried chicken, and this was a good one. Fried chicken, especially spicy fried chicken, is almost an automatic 5.0/5 for me as long as it's crisp, seasoned, and not greasy. Two of us loved it (5.0/5), while two turned out not to like dark meat or thought it was too spicy.


## Nasi katok (fried chicken with rice and sambal)

- Serves: 4 (1 piece of chicken and about 1 cup of rice each)
- Time: About 1½ hours, plus brining the chicken the night before

## Ingredients and directions

### Fried Chicken

### Ingredients

#### Chicken

- 4–5 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs or drumsticks
- Vegetable oil, for frying

#### Dry brine

- 1½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp black pepper

#### Egg wash

- 2 eggs
- 2 tbsp water

#### Coating

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ½ cup cornstarch
- ½ tsp salt
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp black pepper

> **Know before you start:** The chicken dry-brines in the fridge overnight, so start it the night before.

### Directions

1. The night before, pat the chicken thoroughly dry with paper towels. Rub the chicken with the dry brine mixture. You can make a few shallow cuts into the meat to help the seasoning soak in. Place the chicken on a rack or plate, uncovered, in the refrigerator overnight. This seasons the meat and dries the surface for a crispier crust.
2. When you're ready to cook, whisk the eggs with the water. In a separate bowl, mix the flour, cornstarch, salt, garlic powder, and pepper.
3. Dip each piece of chicken into the egg mixture, letting the excess drip off, then press it firmly into the flour mixture. Make sure the chicken is well coated.
4. Heat the oil in a pan to about 325°F. Use enough oil to cover the chicken. Fry until cooked through, about 10–15 minutes. Transfer the chicken to paper towels to drain and rest for 5–10 minutes.
5. Increase the oil temperature to 375°F. Fry the chicken again for about 2 minutes, turning once, until the coating is golden and extremely crisp. Place the chicken on paper towels until ready to eat.

### Sambal

### Ingredients

- 12 dried árbol chiles
- 3 dried guajillo chiles
- 3 medium shallots, roughly chopped
- 5 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 1 stalk lemongrass, tough outer layers removed, finely chopped
- 1½ tbsp anchovy paste
- 1½ tbsp brown sugar
- 5 tbsp vegetable oil
- 2–4 tbsp water, as needed
- Salt, to taste

### Directions

1. Place the árbol and guajillo chiles in a bowl and cover with boiling water. Let them soak for 15–20 minutes, until completely softened. Drain well, remove the stems, and roughly chop.
2. Add the softened chiles, shallots, garlic, lemongrass, and anchovy paste to a blender or food processor. Blend until you have a thick, mostly smooth paste, adding a tablespoon or two of water only if needed to get it moving. A little texture is fine.
3. Heat the vegetable oil in a skillet over medium-low heat. Add the paste and cook for 25–30 minutes, stirring frequently. The mixture will get darker and lose its raw smell, and the oil will begin to separate and pool around the edges. If the paste gets too thick or starts sticking, add water 1 tablespoon at a time. Keep it thick, since you don't want a watery sambal.
4. Stir in the brown sugar and cook for another 5 minutes, stirring frequently.
5. Taste and add salt as needed. The anchovy paste already has salt, so add it gradually.
6. Cook for another 2–3 minutes, then remove from the heat. Let the sambal cool slightly before serving.

### Coconut Rice

### Ingredients

- 2 cups jasmine rice
- 1 cup unsweetened coconut milk
- 2 cups water
- 1 lemongrass stalk, smashed
- ½ tsp salt

### Directions

1. Rinse the rice, then add it to a pot with the rest of the ingredients.
2. Bring to a boil, then cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes. Remove from the heat and fluff with a fork.
