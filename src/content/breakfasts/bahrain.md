## Our choice for Bahrain

Balaleet is a sweet noodle dish topped with eggs. The noodles are cooked with sugar, saffron, cardamom, and rose water, then served under a thin omelet. It's a favorite breakfast for holidays and family weekends across the Gulf. Expect a sweet, perfumed noodle dish with a savory egg on top. It sounds odd, but the sweet and salty combination is the point. I used angel hair pasta broken into short pieces.

## At our table

This dish is very easy to make and very good, provided you don't include the rose water. The rose water is traditional, but I do not like floral tastes in my food. I'd give it a 4.2/5 without the rose water but a 2.5/5 with it. Not a bad meal to make when you want something fast and different.

## Breakfast context: Bahrain

Bahrain is a small island country in the Persian Gulf, connected to Saudi Arabia by a long bridge. For thousands of years it was a trading and pearl-diving center. The food mixes Arab, Persian, and Indian cooking, with lots of rice, dates, fish, and spices like saffron and cardamom. Those spices came in through the trade routes that ran through the Gulf, and they show up in both sweet and savory dishes.


## Recipe

### Vermicelli

#### Ingredients

- ½ tsp saffron threads or powder
- ¼ cup rose water or another flavored water
- 3 tbsp vegetable oil, divided
- 2 cups vermicelli (I used angel hair pasta broken into 1-inch pieces)
- 1 tbsp butter
- ½ cup sugar
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp cardamom powder
- Pinch of black pepper
- Pinch of salt

#### Directions

1. Dissolve the saffron in the flavored water and set aside to steep for 10 minutes. Boil about 4–5 cups of water.
2. Heat 2 tbsp of the vegetable oil over medium-high heat in a pot or deep skillet. Add the noodles and toast, stirring, until lightly browned.
3. Pour the boiling water over the noodles until submerged. Boil until you reach the tenderness you like, per the package instructions. Drain the noodles.
4. Add the butter and the remaining 1 tbsp oil to the pan over medium heat. Once hot, add the noodles back to the pan along with the saffron water, sugar, turmeric, cardamom, black pepper, and salt.
5. Cook until the sugar is dissolved and the water has evaporated.

Note: Rose water is traditional, but ¼ cup water with ¼ tsp vanilla works even better. Plain water with no flavoring also works. Vermicelli is hard to find, but angel hair pasta works as a substitute.

### Eggs

#### Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 1 tbsp milk
- ½ tbsp butter
- Pinch of saffron (optional)
- Pinch of cardamom
- Salt and pepper, to taste

#### Directions

1. Whisk the eggs, milk, salt, cardamom, saffron, and pepper together in a bowl.
2. Heat the butter in a skillet. Cook the eggs until firm.
3. Serve the noodles topped with the eggs.
