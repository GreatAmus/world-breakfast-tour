## The dish

Draniki are hash brown pancakes. They're served with sour cream and chives. You can, optionally, put an egg on top to add even more protein and flavor.

## What we thought

I liked them better than normal hash browns. The egg held them together better, and the onion enhances the taste.

## Rating

4.5/5

## Ingredients and directions

### Ingredients

- 2 lb Idaho potatoes, peeled and grated
- ½ yellow onion, grated
- 2 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp Greek yogurt or sour cream
- 1 egg
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp pepper
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- ½ tsp paprika
- Vegetable oil, for frying

### Directions

1. Squeeze the water out of the potatoes using a paper towel. The less water they have, the better they'll fry.
2. Mix all of the other ingredients in with the squeezed potatoes.
3. Add the oil to a pan and heat it until it's ready for frying. Toss in a piece of potato. If it sizzles, the oil is hot enough. You can use as much or as little oil as you want. I didn't want it deep fried, so I filled the pan until the oil was ⅛ inch deep. Use more if you want it closer to deep fried.
4. Take about ⅓ cup of the potato mix and shape it into a pancake. Place it in the oil and flatten it with a spatula until it's about ¼ inch thick and 4 inches across.
5. Fry until golden brown. Flip the pancake over and fry the other side.
6. Remove from the oil and drain on a paper towel. Season with salt and pepper to taste, then serve with hot sauce, ketchup, or sour cream.
