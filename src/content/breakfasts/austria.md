## Our choice for Austria

Kaiserschmarrn, or "the emperor's mess," is a thick, fluffy pancake that gets torn into pieces in the pan and covered in powdered sugar. Legend says it was a favorite of Emperor Franz Joseph, and it's still served everywhere from mountain huts to cafés. It's usually eaten with stewed plums, so that's what I did. Expect a light, fluffy, slightly caramelized pancake with a tart fruit sauce.

## At our table

The torn pancakes have a taste and texture like an angel food cake and a German pancake had a baby. The sour plum sauce perfectly counters the sweetness of the pancakes.

## Breakfast context: Austria

Austria is in the Alps in central Europe. For centuries Vienna was the capital of a huge empire, and its cooks picked up dishes from Hungary, Bohemia, and the Balkans. Austrians eat a lot of meat, dumplings, and potatoes, but they're most famous for pastries and coffee houses. Dairy and fruit from the mountain farms show up in a lot of desserts.


## Recipe

### Stewed Plums (Zwetschgenröster)

#### Ingredients

- 2 cups pitted, quartered plums (fresh or frozen)
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 star anise
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp water

#### Directions

1. Combine all the ingredients in a small saucepan. Simmer over medium-low heat 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the plums break down into a jammy compote.
2. Remove the cinnamon stick and set aside. Serve warm or at room temperature.

### Kaiserschmarrn

#### Ingredients

- 4 large eggs, separated
- 4 tbsp sugar, divided in half
- 3 tbsp sour cream
- 1 cup whole milk
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- ¼ tsp salt
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- ¼ tsp almond extract (optional)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp raisins, with enough apple juice or rum to cover them (optional)
- 2 tbsp powdered sugar (optional)

#### Directions

1. Soak the raisins in apple juice or rum while you prep the batter.
2. Whisk the egg yolks with half the sugar, the milk, sour cream, vanilla, almond extract, and salt until smooth. Sift in the flour and whisk until combined.
3. Whip the egg whites with the remaining sugar to soft peaks.
4. Gently fold the whipped whites into the batter. Don't overmix. You want this batter light and fluffy, similar to how angel food cake looks.
5. Drain the raisins. Press out any extra liquid between two paper towels.
6. Heat the butter in a large nonstick or cast iron pan over medium heat. Pour in the batter when the butter is hot and sizzling. Sprinkle the raisins over the batter.
7. Cook over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until the bottom is golden and set.
8. Flip the pancake and cook another 3–4 minutes to set the other side.
9. Using two forks, tear the pancake into bite-sized, irregular pieces right in the pan. Cook a few more minutes until the pancake isn't runny but is still light and fluffy.
10. While it's still in the hot pan, sprinkle with powdered sugar.
11. Plate and dust with more powdered sugar. Serve with the warm stewed plums.

Note: I personally hate raisins, so I skipped them completely. Raisins are traditional, though. I like the taste of almonds a lot, so I add the almond extract.
