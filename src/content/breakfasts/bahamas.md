## About the Bahamas

The Bahamas is a chain of about 700 islands in the Atlantic, southeast of Florida. Most Bahamians are descended from enslaved Africans, and the islands were a British colony until 1973. Seafood is the center of the food, especially conch, along with peas and rice and lots of lime and hot peppers. The islands are surrounded by shallow, warm water, so fish and shellfish have always been the easiest protein to get.

## The Dish

Chicken souse is a clear, peppery soup with chicken, potatoes, onions, and a lot of lime juice. It's a traditional weekend breakfast in the Bahamas, and people swear by it after a late night. It's served with johnny cake, a dense, slightly sweet bread baked in a pan. Expect a light, sour, spicy broth that's more about lime and pepper than richness.

## Review

This one was interesting because one kid loved the chicken but hated the johnny cake. The other liked the johnny cake but hated the chicken. Personally, I thought the johnny cake tasted like a standard biscuit and the chicken souse didn't have enough flavor. I used a serrano instead of a habanero, and the kids still complained about the heat. I think I would have liked it a lot more if I'd made it spicy enough that the kids wouldn't have eaten it.


## Chicken souse and johnny cake (lime chicken soup with sweet bread)

- Serves: 6 (about 2 cups of soup and 1 slice of johnny cake each)
- Time: About 1½ hours

## Ingredients and directions

### Chicken Souse

### Ingredients

- 3 lb chicken wings
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 lb russet or Yukon Gold potatoes, cut into cubes
- 4 celery stalks, chopped
- 1 yellow onion, chopped
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 1–2 small hot peppers (depending on how spicy you like it)
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 3 limes, juiced
- ½ tbsp allspice
- 1 bay leaf
- ½ tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and pepper

### Directions

1. Boil the chicken in a pot of water for 10 minutes. The water will look foamy. Drain the wings and rinse them off with water.
2. Rinse the foam out of the pot and return it to the stove. Heat the butter over medium heat. Once hot, add the potatoes.
3. Cook the potatoes for 3 minutes, stirring frequently.
4. Add the celery, carrots, garlic, and onion. Add the allspice and red pepper flakes. Season the veggies with salt and pepper to taste, about 1 tsp salt and ½ tsp pepper. Cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.
5. Add the hot peppers and chicken back to the pot. Cover with just enough water to submerge the wings.
6. Add the lime juice and bay leaf, plus salt to taste, about another 2 tsp.
7. Stir the soup. Once boiling, reduce the heat to medium and cover. Simmer for 30 minutes or until the potatoes are fork tender and the chicken is cooked through.

Note: This recipe depends a lot on how much spice you want. Most recipes recommend a habanero. That would be way too hot for my kids. I recommend a hotter pepper if you like spicy food, as that would have improved this recipe a lot.

### Johnny Cake

### Ingredients

- 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 3 tbsp baking powder
- ½ tsp salt
- 2 eggs
- ⅓ cup sugar
- ½ stick unsalted butter, softened
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 cup water
- 2 tbsp melted butter, for glazing

### Directions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Grease a 9×11 inch baking pan and set aside.
2. Mix the eggs, sugar, and butter together. Then add the milk, oil, and water.
3. Add the flour, baking powder, and salt, mixing until there are no lumps. The consistency should be halfway between a bread dough and cake batter, slightly closer to cake batter. Add more water if necessary.
4. Pour the batter into the greased pan and bake for 30 minutes. Brush the melted butter over the top and bake another 15 minutes, or until golden brown and a butter knife comes out clean from the middle.
5. Serve warm with butter or jam.
