# World Breakfast Tour — remaining editorial notes

All 13 originally flagged priority reviews have been expanded from your own notes. This file now reflects your decisions: one overall family score, estimated timing, plain country and dish background, recipe notes for unusual ingredients, and no separate make-again metric.

## Decisions applied

- **Rating method:** Average the adults separately from the kids, then combine with approximately 60% adult weight and 40% kid weight. The About page explains that this is a rough family process and that the recorded scores were not perfectly consistent. Historical overall scores are unchanged.
- **What scores mean:** Under 2.5 means we wouldn’t eat it again; 2.5–3.9 means we would eat it again but probably wouldn’t put it in the breakfast rotation; 4.0–5.0 means we plan to make it when we need a good breakfast idea. These preserve the site’s existing color cutoffs and place 4.0 in the highest band.
- **No separate group scores:** Adult/kid metadata and leaderboard views have been removed. No old votes need to be reconstructed. Individual reactions in your reviews are retained.
- **No separate make-again field:** The overall score already communicates that decision.
- **Recipe authorship:** The recipes are your own versions, developed from a mix of ideas and recipes. There is no requested single-source recipe credit.
- **Location and dish descriptions:** All 36 posts retain at least four sentences about the location and a full paragraph about the dish (at least three sentences). External reading links have been removed.

- **Timing:** Hands-on, cooking, elapsed, and waiting estimates have been added from the written methods. Hands-on includes attended cooking and overlaps with cooking time; components may cook concurrently. Batch sizes and equipment will affect actual times. Multi-day preparation is explicitly separated from serving-day work.
- **No shopping section:** Unusual ingredients and substitutions stay in the relevant recipe notes as needed. No store names or product links are requested.
